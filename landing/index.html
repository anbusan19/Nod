01 · How it works

Four steps between an agent and your money.

  1. 01

    The agent proposes

    Any script or bot sends Nod a Solana transaction and a one-line reason. It never holds the key that moves the funds.

  2. 02

    Your phone decodes it

    The app reads the real transaction bytes and simulates them locally, so you see what will happen, not what the agent says will happen.

  3. 03

    You give your Nod

    Approve with your fingerprint or face, or your screen lock. Reject, or let it expire, and nothing moves.

  4. 04

    Your wallet signs

    Signing happens in your own wallet through Mobile Wallet Adapter. The key never enters Nod or the backend.

02 · What you see

Read what it really does.

Wallet screens show opaque bytes, and people tap approve without knowing what they sign. Nod turns the transaction into plain language before you decide.

  • Decoded from the transaction itself, not from the agent's description
  • Simulated on your phone, with your balance before and after
  • The agent's stated intent is checked against what the transaction does
  • A countdown, and a fresh check if the blockhash or quote goes stale

03 · Your limits

You decide how much trust an agent gets.

Limits are set per agent, in the app, and enforced before anything reaches your phone.

Auto

Small and expected

Under the amount you set, to recipients you allowed, with known programs only. It goes through without a prompt and shows up in your activity feed.

Nod

Everything else

Larger amounts, new recipients, swaps. Your phone buzzes, shows the decoded card, and waits for your biometric approval.

Block

Never, no matter what

Unknown programs, authority changes, or anything over the hard cap is refused automatically, and the agent is told why.

  • Daily spend cap
  • Allowed recipients
  • Freeze an agent in one tap
  • Revoke keys any time

04 · On your phone

Built for the thumb, not the desk.

Biometric approval

The Android system prompt, with your screen lock as a fallback. Nod never sees biometric data.

Push that says almost nothing

Notifications carry only a proposal id. The details are fetched and decoded on the device.

Your wallet, your keys

Mobile Wallet Adapter signing with Phantom, Solflare or Seed Vault on a Seeker. Keys stay in the wallet.

Hold to approve

A deliberate press instead of a stray tap, with haptic feedback and a visible countdown.

Local simulation

The phone simulates the exact bytes it is about to sign, so the backend is a messenger, not a source of truth.

Activity you can filter

Every proposal, approved, rejected, blocked or expired, by agent and by status.

05 · For developers

Connect any agent in a few lines.

Run your own backend with one command, pair your agent with a code from the app, and propose transactions through a small SDK or plain HTTP. Nothing is locked to a hosted service.

  • npx nod-server starts a backend with its own data folder and secret
  • --tunnel gives it a public https address through your own cloudflared or ngrok
  • Retry-safe proposals, an OpenAPI spec, and a conformance suite for other implementations
# run your backend
npx nod-server --tunnel

// propose a payment from your agent
const nod = new Nod({ apiUrl, agentKey });
const built = await transferSol({ connection, from, to, sol: 0.05 });

const result = await nod.proposeAndWait({
  ...built,
  reason: "Weather API credits",
});

// your phone buzzes; once you nod:
if (isConfirmed(result)) console.log(result.signature);
